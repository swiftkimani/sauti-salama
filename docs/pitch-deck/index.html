The problem

Violence at home is common. Reporting it is almost impossible.

40%+

of ever-partnered Kenyan women aged 15–49 have experienced physical or sexual violence from a partner

34%

of women have experienced physical violence since age 15; 13–14% sexual violence

72h

The barriers are practical: the abuser controls the phone, there is no airtime or data, no private moment, and nobody to call who can come quickly.

Privacy & trust by design

Built for the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 from day one.

No registration, no ID

Anonymity is the difference between a report and no report.

Encrypted at rest

Phone numbers and statements use AES-256-GCM; the console shows masked numbers.

Consent before contact

A number is revealed only if the survivor said the phone is safe, and every reveal is logged.

Real erasure

Erase everything from a free USSD session; closed cases purge after 90 days. Deliberately no blockchain.

Abuse protection without ID checks: per-number rate limiting adds extra reports to the existing case, so a real emergency is never dropped.

Trust · verification · privacySauti Salama
