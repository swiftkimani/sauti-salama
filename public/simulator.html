USSD menu

Silent, works on any phone without data, and leaves nothing in the call log or inbox.

Fastest path: danger alert

  1. Press Dial.
  2. Send 1 for English or 2 for Kiswahili.
  3. Send 2: I am in danger now.
  4. Type an area such as Dandora, or 0 to skip.

Main menu

  • 1Report what happened (5 short questions)
  • 2I am in danger now
  • 3Help information
  • 4Request a call back
  • 5Case status (needs the reference)
  • 6Delete my report (needs the reference)

Recording: what does the caller say?

Call line

For anyone who can talk but may not read or type. Prompts appear as the caller would hear them; press keys when the line asks.

Leave a recorded report

  1. Press Call, then 1 for English or 2 for Kiswahili.
  2. Press 1 to tell the line what happened.
  3. Pick a sample or type a statement, then Finish recording.
  4. Answer the safety question: 1 safe to contact, 2 not safe.

Main menu keys

  • 1Tell us what happened (recorded)
  • 2Hear urgent help information
  • 3Speak to a counsellor or get a call back
  • 9Silent alert: hang up and alert a responder quietly

Tip: while the line is waiting for a key, you can also press digits on your keyboard.
